The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog.
The dog barks, and the fox runs away.
A fox is quick. A dog is lazy.
The QUICK brown fox, the LAZY brown dog.
Foxes chase foxes; dogs chase dogs.
Over the hill and over the river, the fox runs.
The dog sleeps. The fox jumps. The dog sleeps again.
Brown foxes, brown dogs, and the quick brown fox.
